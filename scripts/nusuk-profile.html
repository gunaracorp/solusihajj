SAUDIA UMRAH AND HAJJ

About Us

With over 80 years of experience in serving and transporting pilgrims worldwide, Saudia, through Saudia Hajj & Umrah, stands as a key player in enhancing the pilgrimage journey. Saudia Hajj & Umrah offers a premium travel experience through comprehensive packages that include flight bookings, accommodation, catering, transportation, and visits to Islamic heritage sites. These services are designed to enhance the spiritual journey, complemented by inflight entertainment offering educational and faith-based content to help pilgrims perform their rituals with ease and peace of mind. To date, Saudia Hajj & Umrah has successfully transported over 4 million Umrah pilgrims during the 1445H Umrah season, demonstrating its commitment to excellence in the pilgrimage sector and actively contributing to Saudi Vision 2030’s goal of transporting 30 million Umrah pilgrims annually.
